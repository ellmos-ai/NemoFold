# NemoFold Nautilus Master-Art Prompts

These are the normalized production prompts used with OpenAI built-in image generation. All three master images were generated without text; exact typography and the reusable `NemoFold` lockup are added with a fixed, same-host-reproducible layout by `generate_nautilus_assets.py`.

## Nautilus Descent

- **Use case:** `stylized-concept`
- **Asset:** text-free 16:9 cinematic master for YouTube and Devpost
- **Scene:** an original Victorian-futurist deep-sea submarine evoking the literary Nautilus descends diagonally into a midnight-teal archive trench; Captain Nemo appears as a small dignified silhouette inside a warm observation window; folded document sheets and a restrained coral return route suggest a controlled private archive.
- **Composition:** submarine on the right-center, strong downward silhouette, left 42 percent dark and uncluttered for later title and wordmark.
- **Style:** premium cinematic editorial illustration, restrained painterly realism with crisp geometric technology accents.
- **Palette:** deep teal, cyan, warm ivory, restrained coral, and emerald.
- **Constraints:** no text, letters, logos, watermark, UI, readable document writing, extra people, weapons, or resemblance to a specific film adaptation.
